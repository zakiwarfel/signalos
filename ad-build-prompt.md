# SignalOS ad-build prompt — on demand, not scheduled

Used when Todd picks a concept from `recommendations.md` (or brings his own brief off the research) and wants an actual draft script or copy. This is never part of the automated weekly job — Todd chooses the concept and needs to be involved (he's on camera for UGC ads, and testimonial angles need a real client picked).

## Inputs to confirm first

1. Which concept from `recommendations.md` — or a freeform brief, if he's improvising off the research directly
2. Which business it's for: Enneagram at Work or StackOS/NimblyStack (voice, positioning, and funnel destination differ)
3. Which of the 7 ad types: UGC talking-head, static, features, offer, educational, testimonial, time-sensitive
4. Funnel stage/destination (cold traffic to the free webinar, retargeting, a tripwire product, etc.)
5. If testimonial: which real client and which real result to lead with — never invent one

## Voice

Pull from Todd's voice-guide.md / voice-profile.md (CoWork's `/About Me/` folder). Direct, conversational, story-first. Rule of three. Short sentences, one idea each. No em dashes. No filler words ("really," "actually," "literally," "honestly"). No corporate jargon ("strategic," "leverage," "robust"). Assumptive closing language ("when" not "can we"). Never ends with a thank-you.

## Structure (UGC/talking-head baseline — adapt per ad type)

Hook → setup/story → friction → three main points → resolution → CTA.

- Ground the hook and structure explicitly in whichever competitor pattern it's drawing from, and say so in the deliverable (the same way the recommendation cited its evidence) — this is what makes it a SignalOS output and not a generic copywriting pass.
- Never invent a client story, stat, or credential that isn't already Todd's own. If a detail is needed and isn't already on file, ask rather than fabricate.

## Output

A single draft script/copy file, explicitly framed as a first draft for Todd to cut, reorder, or throw out — never presented as finished. Include a short "why this hook" section citing the specific competitor evidence, same as the sample script built 2026-09-12.
