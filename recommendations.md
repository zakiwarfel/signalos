# SignalOS recommendations

_Last updated: 2026-09-14 · First real run (baseline cycle) · 65 active ads tracked across 13 advertisers_

## What's working right now

**Free diagnostic lead magnets, not high-ticket pitches, are the default door-opener.** RLim Leadership Coaching's "5 reflection questions" guide, EntreLeadership's "Key Results Area" template and "Stages of Business Assessment," and On Call COO's "5 Must-Track Metrics" guide all use the same shape: a short, specific, free download that diagnoses a problem before any pitch happens. This shows up across three unrelated advertisers spanning both the executive-coaching and fractional-COO categories. **Confidence: cross-validated, high.**

**Personal, confessional UGC talking-head video is the dominant format for practitioner-led (not agency-led) offers.** Marlo Lyons Coaching, Double U Advisors, Ed Rush, and Brendon Burchard's GrowthDay funnel all lean on a founder speaking straight to camera with an against-the-grain or vulnerable opener ("I probably shouldn't be saying this as an executive coach...", "Don't we all just hate the first payday that isn't one?", "Most coaches don't fail because they're bad at coaching..."). This is the single most common creative shape in this cycle's dataset. **Confidence: cross-validated, high.**

**Certification-plus-directory is the fractional-leadership business model with the most staying power.** Run Like Clockwork's directory ad has been running essentially unchanged for close to a year (355 days) -- the longest-running ad found this cycle by a wide margin. Center for Executive Coaching and Vanguard XXI both pair certification with an ongoing practitioner/coaching relationship rather than a one-off course. This model is also the one structurally closest to Todd's own certify-coaches approach. **Confidence: cross-validated, high, and directly relevant to Todd's own positioning.**

**Low-price ($47) tripwires are appearing inside otherwise free-training funnels.** Double U Advisors' Executive to Fractional Leader Blueprint and Brendon Burchard's 3-module Life Coaching Class are both $47 offers sitting downstream of free content, rather than the more common free-guide-into-sales-call pattern. Only two advertisers are doing this so far. **Confidence: single-pattern early signal, not yet market-validated -- worth testing, not yet a proven norm.**

## New this week

This is the first real run of the tracker, so there is no prior cycle to diff against -- everything below is a new baseline rather than a week-over-week change. Two things stood out enough to flag on their own:

- **On Call COO (Melissa Franks) launched a brand-new, heavily multi-angle campaign in the last 24-48 hours** -- 8+ fresh ad variants (segmented by $1M+ vs. $3M+ revenue, and by pain point: loneliness at the top, bottleneck founder, hidden profit, operations outrunning growth) all pushing the same "message PARTNER" CTA. This is a live experiment in progress; next cycle's diff will show which angle(s) survive and which get killed.
- **Enneagram MBA and The COO Solution both came back empty this cycle** -- Enneagram MBA's search surfaced only unrelated ads from an unaffiliated advertiser ("CRG - Consulting Resources"), and The COO Solution's name is too generic to search reliably on Meta's Ad Library (see run notes). Neither should be read as "this advertiser stopped advertising" yet -- both need a manual spot-check before drawing that conclusion.

## Suggested ad concepts

**1. UGC talking-head, StackOS/NimblyStack.** Premise: a confessional, against-the-grain video in Todd's own voice -- something like "I probably shouldn't say this as a certification provider, but most certifications don't actually get you your first client." Draws on the confessional-UGC pattern (cross-validated across 4 advertisers) and the low-price-tripwire pattern, and would set up a $47-or-similar tripwire the way Double U Advisors and Brendon Burchard do.

**2. Offer ad (free lead magnet), Enneagram at Work.** Premise: a short, specific diagnostic download -- e.g. "5 Signs Your Team's [Enneagram-type] Conflict Is Actually a Values Mismatch" -- mirroring the free-diagnostic-guide shape that's working across RLim, EntreLeadership, and On Call COO. This is the most cross-validated pattern in the dataset and currently has no direct match in the Enneagram at Work competitive set specifically, since Enneagram MBA's own ads didn't surface this cycle.

**3. Features ad, StackOS/NimblyStack.** Premise: lead with the certified-practitioner directory/community itself as the product, the way Run Like Clockwork does -- positioning StackOS/NimblyStack's certified coaches as a vetted, ongoing resource rather than a one-time course. This is the closest structural match to an ad that has proven durable (355 days running) in a directly comparable business model.

## Run notes

- **The COO Solution** and a plain **"GrowthDay"** search both returned 50,000+ largely unrelated results ("COO," "Solution," and "Growth" are common substrings) -- flagged as too generic to search reliably by name alone. Brendon Burchard's GrowthDay-linked ads were still captured via the "Brendon Burchard" query, which returned clean, exact-name matches.
